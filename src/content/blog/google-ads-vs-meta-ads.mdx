"Should we spend our marketing budget on Google Ads or Facebook and Instagram ads?" It is one of the most common questions we hear from business owners. The honest answer is: **it depends on your customers, your offer and where they are in their buying journey.**

This guide explains how each platform works, where each one shines and how to decide the right mix for your business.

## How Google Ads works

Google Ads is **intent-based**. Your ads appear when people actively search for what you offer — for example "website development company in Delhi" or "CA near me". You are reaching people at the exact moment they are looking for a solution.

Google Ads covers several networks:

- **Search** — text ads on Google search results
- **Performance Max and Shopping** — product listings and automated campaigns across Google
- **Display** — banner ads across millions of partner websites
- **YouTube** — video ads before and during videos

Because searchers already have a need, Google Ads is excellent for generating high-quality leads and sales quickly.

## How Meta Ads works

Meta Ads run across **Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp**. Instead of keywords, you target people based on demographics, interests, behaviours and lookalike audiences built from your existing customers.

Meta Ads are **discovery-based**: people are not searching for you, so your creative has to earn their attention. That makes Meta ideal for building awareness, launching new products and creating demand for things people did not know they needed.

## Google Ads vs Meta Ads at a glance

| | Google Ads | Meta Ads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How people find you** | They search for it (high intent) | You find them while they scroll |
| **Targeting** | Keywords, location, device, audiences | Demographics, interests, behaviours, lookalikes |
| **Best for** | Capturing existing demand, urgent needs | Creating demand, visual products, brand awareness |
| **Ad formats** | Text, shopping, display, video | Images, videos, carousels, reels, stories |
| **Creative needs** | Relevant copy and landing pages | Eye-catching visuals and video |
| **Funnel stage** | Bottom of funnel (ready to buy) | Top and middle of funnel (awareness, consideration) |

## When Google Ads is the better choice

Choose Google Ads first when:

- People already search for your product or service
- Customers have an **urgent need** — repairs, legal help, healthcare, B2B services
- You sell high-value services where a single lead is worth a lot
- You want to capture enquiries in a specific city or area, such as Delhi NCR

## When Meta Ads is the better choice

Choose Meta Ads first when:

- Your product is **visual** — fashion, food, home décor, real estate, travel
- You are launching something new that people are not yet searching for
- You want to build a brand and an engaged community
- Your offer suits impulse or lifestyle purchases

## Why the best answer is often both

The strongest campaigns use both platforms together, each doing what it does best:

1. **Meta Ads** introduce your brand to new audiences and build interest.
2. Interested people later **search for you on Google** — and your Google Ads capture them.
3. **Remarketing** on both platforms reminds visitors who did not convert the first time.

## How to decide your budget split

There is no universal formula, but a sensible approach is:

- **Start where intent is highest.** If people search for your service, begin with Google Search.
- **Test before you scale.** Run small, focused campaigns on each platform for a few weeks.
- **Track what matters.** Measure cost per lead and cost per sale — not likes, impressions or clicks.
- **Shift budget to what works.** Review results monthly and move spend towards the best performers.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Sending ad traffic to your home page instead of a focused landing page
- Targeting too broadly — or too narrowly — without testing
- Judging campaigns after only a few days
- Ignoring mobile users (a slow mobile page wastes ad spend — see our guide to [must-have website features](/blog/business-website-must-have-features))
- Running ads without clear offers or calls to action

## Conclusion

Google Ads captures demand; Meta Ads creates it. If your customers are already searching, start with Google. If you need to be discovered, start with Meta. And once you have proven what works, combining both — with proper tracking — is how you build a predictable flow of leads.
